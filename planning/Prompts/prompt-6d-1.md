Patients = number of those patients; Visits = all visits in that category and period. Sex, age bracket, diagnosis and branch/rank come from each patient's latest visit (sex from the patient). A patient counts once per category per period, so a patient with ER visits in January and February counts once in Q1 ER. With Category = All, also show the grand totals: patients counted once across all categories, and total visits.
4. Sections per category, in this order, as the prototype: Patients / Visits; Sex (Male, Female, Total); Age range (each active bracket in From-age order, plus a "No bracket" line only when some age fits none); Diagnosis (Top 10): sorted by count, then name; "Other (specify)" visits and everything after the 10th go into one "Others" line, shown last; Branch of service and rank: each branch with patients, in branch order, with its total, then its ranks with patients in the Lists seniority order (sort_order); "Other (specify)" ranks show the typed text as their own lines after the listed ranks. 5. 6. Closed month: for Monthly and a closed month, show the saved report_snapshots copy instead of live counts, with the prototype's note "Saved copy, taken when <month> was closed on <date>. Later changes to lists or records don't affect it." Quarterly, Annual and Custom are always counted live.
